The big statement type — display caps at 0.92 leading and -0.03em tracking. One per view; two at most.

```jsx
<DisplayHeading level={1} as="h1" treatment="inverse">Flow with it</DisplayHeading>
<DisplayHeading level={2} treatment="outline">Selected work</DisplayHeading>
```

`outline` is the hairline knockout used only over photography, per the reference hero. Never mix `outline` and `solid` inside one heading unless intentionally contrasting two words.
