Therma-Shift coaster

A solid state desktop coaster that heats or cools any mug, glass or can.

All work→

Heated mugs lock you into their cup. Cheap USB warmers have no temperature control.

The problem

01

The coaster has to run unattended on a desk.

Constraints

02
01Universal: any mug, glass or can, the device adapts to the drinkware
02Dual mode, heating and cooling from the same hardware
03Powered over USB-C Power Delivery, within a desk power budget
04Thermal runaway prevention independent of the control loop
05Low profile and quiet enough for an office desk
06No proprietary cup, ever

The design

03

A thermoelectric module heats or cools depending on current direction. The TEC1-12703 sits between an aluminum top plate and a finned heatsink with a blower.

An NTC thermistor at the plate closes the loop, targeting about 2 degrees F around setpoint.

Separate firmware cutoffs watch for thermal runaway independently of the control loop and shut the device down on a fault.

Bench test

04

Before adding any control loop, I bench tested the bare thermal stack on a lab supply with K-type thermocouples on both faces.
