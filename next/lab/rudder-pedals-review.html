    Springs and wires hide while the view is exploded.

    What I assumed

    1. Centering springs hook into the 6 mm hole under each lever's top bar, right above the adjustment holes, and onto a screw in the wall's column of nine holes. I put the screw in hole 5 (the STEP has it in hole 1).
    2. Spring sizes are my picks: centering 24 coils, 8.3 mm outside, 1.1 mm wire; brake 13 coils, 5.9 mm outside, 0.9 mm wire.
    3. Brake springs run from a small eye screw on the lever's vertical inner edge to the pin through the pedal's clevis, square to the pin. The eye screw is not in the STEP.
    4. A toe brake tips the pedal back up to 12 degrees on its hinge bolt, which moves the magnet away from the sensor.
    5. Magnets are 10 x 3 mm nickel discs. The round pockets measure 10.2 mm across and 2.8 mm deep.
    6. The left Hall sensor sits at the bottom of the slit in the left lever. The right lever in the STEP has no slit (it looks like an older version), so its sensor sits at the mirrored spot.
    7. Each sensor has three wires (red, black, signal) that run up the slit, out through the notch and down the lever, then hang in a slack loop to the frame. The right side's wires pass through the inner slot of each wall and across between the walls.
    8. The controller board is a 50 x 34 mm perfboard with the Pro Micro on headers, on standoffs on the left wall's outer face below the lever. Its place and size are my guess.
    9. The magnet holder on the sensor lever suggests a third Hall sensor for the rudder axis. I did not add one.
    10. Full rudder swings each lever about 11 degrees (the sensor lever 20 degrees).
