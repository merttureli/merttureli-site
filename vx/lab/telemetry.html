scroll.state.vy, signed, px/s; the runway below gives it room.

transferSize summed over Resource Timing entries; cached and cross-origin entries report 0 B and are counted.
GL
bus.state stage:render; a dash on this page because no stage is mounted.
Input
pointer media query, last pointer:move emitted by this page.
Mem
performance.memory where it exists; a dash elsewhere.

Strips draw at the tier's rate: every frame at T3, 30 Hz at T2, 10 Hz at T1; at T0 the rings fill from governor:stats at 4 Hz and the canvas repaints when the scroll stops. The loop runs only while the canvas is on screen.
