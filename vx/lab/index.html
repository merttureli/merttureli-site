The modules, one at a time

The field study is these five modules wired together by js/main.js. Each lab runs one of them alone, with its parameters as controls and its internal state printed beneath. Every lab also passes the page harness at desktop and phone width, with and without WebGL.

  1. 01FlightThe 3-DOF rigid-body flight model with Barrowman stability: scenarios, the event list, an event's state snapshot, the side view with CG, CP and the normal force.js/flight/flight.js, barrowman.js, flight.test.mjs
  2. 02GeometryThe section geometry measured from the CAD assembly: the solid mask at each tier's grid, the fin measurements against the case study's figures, the half-section drawing.js/geom/geom.js, geom-measure.js, geom-cad.js
  3. 03LatticeThe D2Q9 lattice Boltzmann engine on the GPU: the four field views, the force and moment by momentum exchange, and the cylinder validation gate at ?validate=cylinder.js/lattice/lattice.js, shaders.js, lattice-lab.js
  4. 04SceneThe 3D stage: the CAD model in its livery, the rail and the altitude ladder, the slice plane with a test card, the markers, the camera presets and the alpha sign check.js/scene/scene.js, slice.js, markers.js, camera.js, exhaust.js
  5. 05PanelThe instrument panel: readouts with their source tags, the controls, the strip chart, the phone sheet and the 2D view used without WebGL2.js/panel/panel.js, controls.js, chart.js, fallback.js, format.js

Shared: css/tokens.css, js/core/bus.js, js/core/governor.js (quality tiers; ?tier=0..3 pins one), js/stage/loader.js (the assembly loader). The flight tests run under node: node vx/js/flight/flight.test.mjs.
