VXProduction site

Every engine of the vx build runs alone here with its parameters exposed as controls and its internal state printed beneath. The page at /vx/ is these engines wired together.

  1. 01StageThe CHARGER flight model rendered live from the published assembly: pad view, the eight-station descent, the explode schedule, the silhouette.js/stage/
  2. 02CalloutsLeader-line part callouts from the explorer's part stories, solved on two rails or in a bottom sheet, on a synthetic or the real stage.js/callouts.js
  3. 03TunnelThe GPU wind tunnel behind the hero: stable fluids on ping-pong framebuffers with a draggable obstacle and four field views. Page simulation, not flight data.js/tunnel/
  4. 04BootThe T-minus sequence: every awaited step with its measured milliseconds while a pen plotter draws the rocket from the bytes as they arrive.js/boot.js, js/plotter.js
  5. 05InstrumentsAltimeter, vertical speed indicator and engine gauge, reading scroll depth, scroll velocity and frame time. SRC: SCROLL and SRC: RAF.js/instruments.js
  6. 06TelemetryTen cells and two strip charts of page instrumentation: frame time, long tasks, scroll, viewport, network, GL, governor, input, memory.js/telemetry.js
  7. 07ConsoleThe command console with a manifest of the repository's real files and their real byte sizes, fuzzy matching, history and a calendar of the roles.js/console.js, js/data/vfs.js
  8. 08HID benchThree gauges reading any connected controller through the Gamepad API, with the measured poll rate. SRC: GAMEPAD API.js/hid.js
  9. 09Contact sheetA 36-cell contact sheet of 35 frames with a loupe that samples the files at native resolution, a viewer and a PNG export.js/contact-sheet.js, js/data/frames.js
  10. 10DimensionsLive dimension lines in CSS px and millimetres on any element carrying data-dim, with boxed basic dimensions and their deviation.js/dimension.js
  11. 11Timeline and ladderThe mission timeline on a real month axis, the launch weekend on an hour track, and the flight-event ladder with every figure sourced.js/timeline.js, js/ladder.js, js/data/mission.js
Every number on these pages is measured from the page in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere.
