    State / live

    Derived / Barrowman, liftoff mass

    Shape from the CAD assembly (46.5 in, four fins, CAD); mass and CG from the page (2.2 lb, 22 in, PAGE). The page's 1.80 cal is its three-fin design review figure; every margin here is recomputed (SIM). The CAD fin's root chord and sweep match the page's 2.76 in and 2.17 in; its span is 2.17 in against the page's 2.36 in height. The drag coefficient is fitted so the still-air apogee equals the page's 1,033 ft (FIT). Thrust: AeroTech G77R RASP curve from thrustcurve.org via OpenRocket, tagged STD as published reference data. Inertia, propellant station and burnout CG are estimates (EST). Alpha is ill-defined as airspeed goes to zero, so the chart scales to the part of the flight above 50 Pa of dynamic pressure and shades the rest. First alpha peak is the end of the first swing after rail exit (alpha low-passed at 5 Hz, so turbulence noise cannot end it); max alpha in the state readout is the largest over the ascent. The normal-force arrow sits at CP(alpha), the resultant including body lift (hollow ring, SIM); the filled dot is the small-alpha Barrowman CP. Without fins CHARGER does not tumble: body lift acts at the planform centroid, 24.42 in from the nose (SIM), aft of the 22 in CG, so the rocket settles at a high-alpha trim and loops over instead of passing 60 deg of angle of attack.
