Bench

Scripted stand-in, not the flight model. The panel is driven here by a script with the flight module's shape: rail exit at 0.35 s, a gust at 0.60 s and the 1.2 Hz damped wobble of angle of attack after it, burnout at 1.25 s, apogee near 8 s. Its numbers show that the panel reads, converts and tags values correctly; they are not results. The lattice line and lattice CP are scripted the same way. The 2D view on the stage is the panel's fallback for browsers without WebGL2, drawn from an inline polygon set (ogive nose, body tube, two trapezoidal fins, metres).
