# Track L2: cylinder series and domain effects

Validation campaign for the CHARGER field study lattice (VALIDATION.md, track L2). Engine vx/js/lattice/lattice.js (sha256 prefix b19ab848f2e44159) and shaders.js (185ad9e3851e3b51), branch claude/charming-pascal-wgx7a3. Runs 2026-10-23. Lattice units throughout (one cell, one step, c_s^2 = 1/3); coefficients use the inflow speed and the nominal diameter in cells, C = F / (0.5 rho_ref u_in^2 D) with rho_ref = 1.

## Summary

- The engine converges to the published 2D cylinder values once the grid and the domain are large enough. With the domain effects removed and the grid increment to the finest grid added, Re 100 gives C_D 1.318 (reference 1.320, -0.1 %), St 0.1606 (-2.4 %), C_L amplitude 0.339 (+6.5 %); Re 200 gives C_D 1.333 (+0.2 %), St 0.1909 (-3.6 %), C_L amplitude 0.691 (+0.8 %); steady Re 20 C_D 1.997 (-2.3 %), L_r/D 0.935 (+1.1 %); Re 40 C_D 1.501 (-2.8 %), L_r/D 2.324 (+2.8 %).
- At gate-like settings (D 16 to 24 cells, inflow edge 5 to 8 D ahead, lateral edges 4 D away) it reads high: Re 100 C_D 1.460 at D 16, H 8 D (+10.6 %), and the gate's own geometry gives a saturated C_D 1.473 (+11.6 %) and St 0.1720 (+4.6 %).
- The largest single error source is the distance to the inflow edge, not blockage. The edge fixes u and rho; moving it from 8 D to 16 D ahead lowers C_D by 4.6 percent at Re 100 and 6.1 percent at Re 20; the effect falls off as about (distance)^-2.1. The lateral edges are open at fixed pressure: between 8 D and 32 D of height C_D moves by at most 1.4 percent at Re 100 and 1.2 percent at Re 200, not monotonically, and it rises with height at Re 20 and 40 (6.5 and 4.3 percent; an open-jet effect), so closed-wall blockage corrections do not apply.
- The gate printed on the page (C_D 1.450, St 0.169, after 20,000 steps) is reproduced to every printed digit by the CPU transcription, but it is taken before the shedding saturates (C_L amplitude within 5 percent of its final value only from step 22,308); run to 60,000 steps the same case gives C_D 1.473, St 0.172, C_L amplitude 0.359. The method section's '1.35 to 1.55 and 0.16 to 0.18 for 12 percent blockage' is not a published range for this domain; the statement does not hold as written (section 8).
- Schaefer and Turek 2D-1 (channel, Re 20): with the engine's uniform inflow edge the benchmark's inlet (a parabola imposed 2 D ahead of the cylinder) cannot be reproduced, and C_D lands -3.4 to -4.7 percent off at D 20 to 40 whatever the grid, Mach number or stabilisers. With the benchmark's inlet in a test-only build of the transcription C_D converges: +3.8 percent at D 20, +1.7 percent at D 30, +1.4 percent at D 40, with dp within 0.1 percent and L_a within the benchmark's 10 percent tolerance by D 30 (section 6).
- The developed flag (2,000 steps after a reset, 6 D/u at D 16 and less on finer grids) comes before the forces are stationary in every flow measured here: shedding saturates after 38 to 53 D/u (D 16 to 48), steady drag is within 1 percent after 12 to 51 D/u depending on the domain, and the rocket stand-in's chord-pass means need about 1.5 chord passes (section 9).
- Every lattice number below comes from a single-thread CPU transcription of the engine's step (l2lbm3.c), checked against the engine on SwiftShader: four cases agree to 1e-4 in C_D and C_L, and the 20,000-step gate agrees to all printed digits. The transcription ran at 13 to 41 MCUPS per run on this shared host against 0.6 to 1.5 MCUPS for SwiftShader.

## Method

**What ran.** SwiftShader managed 9 to 17 steps per second on the L2 grids while this host was shared with the other tracks (0.6 to 1.5 MCUPS; probe-rate and the eq cases below), so a 60,000-step case would take one to two hours. The step pass of vx/js/lattice/shaders.js (STEP_FRAG: edges, wall links with the moving-wall term and the Galilean-corrected momentum exchange, BGK plus Smagorinsky, regularisation, speed floor, absorbing layer, positivity limiter) and the host logic of lattice.js that drives it (reset to uniform inflow, start-up viscosity 0.75 easing to tau0 over 2,000 steps, inflow slew 1e-4 per step, tau0 = 0.5 + 3 u D / Re) were transcribed to C in float32 (scratchpad validation/L2/l2lbm3.c, clang -O3 -ffp-contract=off -fno-math-errno, one thread per run, each run in its own session so it gets one scheduler share). The transcription is bit-identical to the gcc build of the first version (l2lbm.c) and agrees with the engine as shown in section 1. GPU runs use vx/lab/validation/l2-cylinder.html through the scratchpad driver run.cjs (Playwright, SwiftShader, analytics and form hosts blocked, no POST); CPU runs use cpu2.cjs and queue2.cjs. Both reduce the traces with vx/js/validation/l2-analysis.js.

**Protocol.** u_in 0.05 (lattice Mach 0.087). Engine defaults unless stated: Smagorinsky C 0.12, regularised collision, positivity limiter, speed floor 2 above |u| 0.15 (never reached here, max |u| 0.076), absorbing layer max(8, 5 percent of rows) cells wide rising to tau 0.9 at every edge. Cylinder: cell centres within D/2 are solid (staircase, halfway bounce-back). Unsteady cases start with the inflow at 4 deg for 5 D/u steps, as the engine gate does, then 0 deg. Forces: momentum exchange summed over the cylinder links each step (a box sum, identical to the engine's whole-domain reduction when the cylinder is the only solid: max relative difference 0). Averages over the last 10 lift cycles that are saturated (mean drag within 0.2 percent and lift amplitude within 5 percent of the mean of the last three cycles); St = D / (T u_in); C_L amplitude = half the peak-to-peak lift per cycle. Mean L_r/D from the time-averaged centre-line u_x (second half of the run), measured from the rear halfway wall to the zero crossing. Steady cases stop when the 2,000-step mean drag changes by less than 2e-5 (1e-4 on D 24 and 32), else at the cap; an Aitken extrapolation in time is shown beside the last value.

**Domain notation.** Blockage beta = D / H. Upstream and downstream distances are from the cylinder centre to the inflow and outflow edges. Series: H 8, 12, 16, 24, 32 D at 8 D upstream and 20 D (unsteady) or 16 D (steady) downstream; upstream 8, 16, 32 D at H 16 D; one 32 D by 32 D domain; grids D 16, 24, 32, 48 on H 8 D, 8 D up, 12 D down (unsteady) and D 16, 24, 32 on H 16 D (steady).

**Correction.** The domain-free value at D 16 is the 32 D by 32 D case plus the remaining upstream effect at 32 D taken from the upstream series (fitted exponent); its uncertainty adds that correction and the change between H 24 and H 32. The grid increment is the change from D 16 to the finest grid on the grid series' domain, added on the assumption that grid and domain effects add. Corrected = domain-free D 16 value + grid increment.

**Tolerances** (set before the runs): C_D 5 percent, St 5 percent, C_L amplitude 15 percent, L_r/D 10 percent of the centre of the cited reference range; GPU against CPU 1e-3 in any force coefficient sample; Schaefer and Turek: C_D 2 percent, L_a 10 percent, dp 5 percent, C_L 30 percent.

Between closed walls continuity would push the flow past a cylinder at beta = 1/8 to at least 1/(1 - beta) = 1.143 u_in. Here the time-averaged speed in the outermost interior rows at the cylinder's column is 1.035 u_in at H 8 D and falls to about 1.00 by H 16 D: the edges let the displaced fluid out. C_D at Re 100 and 200 changes by at most 1.4 and 1.2 percent between H 8 and 32 D and not monotonically; the C_L amplitude grows with height. At Re 20 and 40 C_D grows with height (open-jet behaviour: a constant-pressure boundary close to the body lowers its drag). A wall-blockage correction (for example the one behind the page's citation of Sahin and Owens 2004 for walls) has the wrong sign and size for these edges, so none is applied; the height effect is removed with the 32 D by 32 D case instead.

## 8. Does the method section's statement hold

vx/index.html section 07 says: an unconfined cylinder at Re 100 sheds at St about 0.164 (Williamson 1996), blockage raises drag and frequency (Sahin and Owens 2004), "for 12 percent blockage the gate takes a drag coefficient of 1.35 to 1.55 and a Strouhal number of 0.16 to 0.18", and the gate measures C_D 1.450 and St 0.169, "both inside the ranges".

- The measured numbers are right for what the gate ran (reproduced exactly), but they are pre-saturation: the same case saturates at C_D 1.473 and St 0.172.
- The window 1.35 to 1.55 / 0.16 to 0.18 is not a published range for this domain. Sahin and Owens studied a cylinder between channel walls; the engine's lateral edges are open at fixed pressure, where blockage moves C_D by at most 1.4 percent at Re 100 (section 3.1). The gate's excess over the unconfined values comes from the inflow edge 5.3 D ahead and the grid, not from blockage.
- Against the unconfined 2D references (C_D 1.320, St 0.1645) the saturated gate is +11.6 % in C_D and +4.6 % in St. 'Both inside the ranges' is true of the window and hides an 11 percent drag excess.
- What the page can say instead: at the gate's settings the lattice reads C_D about 11 percent and St about 5 percent high; with the domain and grid effects removed it lands within 0.1 percent (C_D) and 2.4 percent (St) of the published 2D values (section 5).

- CPU transcription, one thread per run, 4 to 5 runs at once on a 4-core host shared with the other tracks: 12.8 to 41.5 MCUPS per run (median 21.6); for example 260 steps/s on 448 x 128 and 56 steps/s on 768 x 512.
- Engine on SwiftShader on the same host: 17.3 steps/s on 448 x 192 (1.49 MCUPS), 9 steps/s on 850 x 86 (0.66 MCUPS), 12.9 steps/s on 384 x 128 (0.63 MCUPS). The integrator's earlier unshared run did 99 steps/s on 512 x 192 (9.7 MCUPS). Software rendering is not representative of a graphics card.

## 11. Defects

1. **vx/js/lattice/lattice-lab.js validateCylinder (the ?validate=cylinder gate)** (moderate). The gate reports C_D and St before the vortex shedding has saturated and over a window that is not a whole number of cycles: C_D is the mean over steps 15,000 to 20,000 (1.8 lift periods), St comes from 4 zero crossings starting at step 8,000, and the lift amplitude reaches 95 percent of its final value only at step 22308. Evidence: Section 2: the CPU transcription reproduces the printed 1.4504 / 0.1694 / 0.319 exactly; the same case run to 60,000 steps saturates at C_D 1.4731, St 0.1720, C_L amplitude 0.359 (printed values -1.5 %, -1.5 %, -11.2 %). Proposed fix: Run until the mean drag and the lift amplitude have stopped growing (about 60,000 steps at D 24 from the current kick) and average over whole saturated cycles, as vx/js/validation/l2-analysis.js now does; print the saturated numbers.

2. **vx/index.html section 07 (data-slot lattice-validation and the sentence before it) and vx/README.md line 110** (moderate). The method section's reference window 'for 12 percent blockage the gate takes a drag coefficient of 1.35 to 1.55 and a Strouhal number of 0.16 to 0.18', justified by wall blockage (Sahin and Owens 2004), is not a published range for this engine's domain, whose lateral edges are open at fixed pressure; the window hides an 11 percent drag excess. Evidence: Section 3.1: C_D changes by at most 1.4 and 1.2 percent between 8 and 32 D of height at Re 100 and 200 and the edge speed at H 8 D is 1.035 u_in, against the 1.143 u_in that walls would force. Section 8: the saturated gate is +11.6 % in C_D and +4.6 % in St against the unconfined 2D references (Parvar et al. 2023, Table 1). Proposed fix: Replace the window with the unconfined 2D references (C_D 1.31 to 1.33, St 0.164 to 0.165 at Re 100) and print the gate as measured against them, with the domain and grid effects stated (sections 3 to 5). Copy changes go to the owner.

3. **vx/js/lattice/lattice-lab.js validateCylinder geometry (cx = 128 in a 512-column grid: inflow edge 5.3 D ahead of a D 24 cylinder); the same inflow-edge condition in shaders.js STEP_FRAG** (moderate). The inflow edge fixes both u and rho one cell from the domain boundary, so a body close to it sees a faster approach flow than an unbounded stream; at 8 D the drag is 6 to 9 percent above its unbounded-upstream value (Re 100 to Re 20) and St 4 percent high at Re 100, and the gate sits closer (5.3 D). Evidence: Section 3.2: Re 100 C_D 1.4589 / 1.3915 / 1.3756 and St 0.1632 / 0.1579 / 0.1567 at 8 / 16 / 32 D upstream (D 16, H 16 D); Re 20 C_D 2.1651 / 2.0341 at 8 / 16 D. The gate (5.3 D) reads C_D 1.4731 against 1.4123 for D 24 at 8 D (re100-d24-h8). Proposed fix: For the gate: put the cylinder 16 D or more behind the inflow edge (for example 1024 x 384 with D 24 at x = 384) or quote the upstream distance with the result. For the page this is part of the documented confinement (lattice.js header, EDGES).

4. **vx/js/lattice/shaders.js STEP_FRAG outer edges (inflow cells written as the equilibrium at rho 1 and u_in when uInflowNeq is 0, the default)** (moderate). An inflow cell set to the equilibrium at rho = 1 does not impose the inflow velocity: the mass it admits per unit height is about u_in - (rho_inside - 1) / 3, so wherever the pressure behind the edge rises the delivered inflow drops. Evidence: Section 6: in the Schaefer and Turek channel (pressure about 1.5 percent above the outlet behind the inflow edge) the default edge delivered U_mean = 0.0437 for u_in 0.05 (87.4 percent), so the benchmark ran at Re 17.5; the engine option inflowNeq delivered 0.0504 (100.8 percent). The same mechanism is behind the lattice.js header's note that only half the imposed cross-flow gets in at incidence. Proposed fix: Use a velocity inlet that lets rho float (the existing inflowNeq option, or Zou and He 1997 non-equilibrium bounce-back, or a regularised inlet) wherever the inflow speed must be exact; keep the equilibrium edge only where it is wanted as a pressure-anchoring boundary, and say which in the method section.

5. **vx/js/lattice/lattice.js STARTUP_STEPS and devRun (force.developed, stats.developed)** (minor). The developed flag is set 2,000 steps after a reset (once the inflow has arrived), which is 6 D/u for a D 16 cylinder and 0.6 chord passes for the rocket section at u 0.10 (0.2 at 0.03); the forces are not stationary then. Evidence: Section 9: shedding saturates after 38 to 53 D/u (D 16 to 48); steady drag is off its final value by up to 41 percent at step 2,000 and within 1 percent only after 12 to 51 D/u; the rocket stand-in's one-pass C_m mean at step 2,000 is 31 to 53 percent below its later mean (tier 1 and tier 3). Proposed fix: Count developed after a convective travel of about 1.5 body lengths since the last reset or alpha change (sum of u_in per step >= 1.5 L), and show C_N and C_m as means over one chord pass.

6. **vx/js/lattice/shaders.js STEP_FRAG wall links (halfway bounce-back on a staircase mask)** (minor). Curved walls are staircased and bounced back halfway between cells, which is first order in the wall position and couples the wall error to tau; at the grids the engine uses the cylinder drag is several percent high and the grid series is not yet asymptotic. Evidence: Section 4: Re 100 C_D 1.4388 / 1.4123 / 1.3853 / 1.3760 at D 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 (no single power law); Re 200 C_D 1.4718 / 1.4483 / 1.3946 / 1.3836. The gate's D 24 sits about 3 percent above the D 48 value on the same domain. Proposed fix: Interpolated bounce-back (Bouzidi, Firdaouss and Lallemand 2001) using the signed distance that vx/js/geom already computes per boundary cell; re-run this series.

Benchmark-runner bugs found and fixed during this track (not engine defects): vx/js/validation/l2-analysis.js averaged the last 10 lift cycles even while the shedding was still growing, which biased the D 32 results low (now only saturated cycles are averaged and the result says saturated: true or false); the channel profile included the two edge rows outside the channel walls (vx/js/validation/l2-cylinder.js and cpu2.cjs now take only the rows between the walls); the CPU runs were first started with OpenMP threads, which stalled on the shared host (17 steps/s), and are now single-thread runs in their own sessions.

## 12. Limits the page should state

- At the gate's own settings (D 24 cells, inflow edge 5.3 D ahead) the Re 100 cylinder reads C_D 1.473 and St 0.172 once saturated: +12 percent and +5 percent against unconfined 2D values (C_D 1.320, St 0.165).
- With domain and grid effects removed the lattice gives Re 100 C_D 1.318 (-0.1 %), St 0.1606 (-2.4 %); Re 200 C_D 1.333 (+0.2 %), St 0.1909 (-3.6 %): drag within about 1 percent, Strouhal number 2 to 4 percent low.
- The inflow edge holds u and rho fixed: 8 D ahead of a cylinder it raises C_D by about 5 percent at Re 100 relative to 16 D, and by about 6 percent at Re 20.
- The lateral edges are open at fixed pressure: domain height moves the Re 100 and 200 drag by at most 1.4 and 1.2 percent between 8 and 32 D (and raises the Re 20 and 40 drag with height), so wall-blockage corrections and blockage-based ranges do not apply.
- Grid: the Re 100 drag falls about 2 percent per step from D 16 to 24 to 32 cells and 0.7 percent from 32 to 48, so the cylinder drag at the 16 to 39 cells the page's sections use carries a grid error of several percent; steady Re 20 and 40 drag converges to 0.3 percent by D 24.
- Forces need 38 to 53 D/u after a reset for a shedding cylinder and 12 to 51 D/u (drag within 1 percent) for steady flow; the 2,000-step developed flag is 6 D/u at D 16.
- The rocket stand-in at alpha 5 deg and Re 3000 sheds: single C_N and C_m readings scatter widely around their means (see section 9), so only means over a chord pass or longer are meaningful, and those settle after about 1.5 chord passes, not after 2,000 steps.
- The default inflow edge admits u_in - (rho - 1)/3: behind a pressure rise of 1.5 percent it delivered 87 percent of u_in (Schaefer and Turek channel).
- Schaefer and Turek 2D-1 needs a parabolic inflow 2 D ahead of the cylinder, which the engine's uniform inflow edge cannot impose: with the edge as it is C_D is -3.4 to -4.7 percent off, L_a about 7 to 8 percent short and dp about 9 to 10 percent low at D 20 to 40; with the benchmark's inlet (test build) C_D is +3.8 percent at D 20, +1.7 percent at D 30, +1.4 percent at D 40.
- The Smagorinsky term and the stabilisers together shift the Re 100 cylinder drag by about 1 percent at D 16; they are not the source of the larger errors.
- Software rendering (SwiftShader) ran the engine at 0.6 to 1.5 MCUPS on the shared host (9 to 17 steps/s on these grids); step and frame rates under software GL are not representative.

## 13. What was not done, and why

- Long GPU runs of every case: SwiftShader on the shared host would need one to two hours per case; the CPU transcription stands in, with the equivalence of section 1 (five cases to about 1e-4, one of them 20,480 steps long, and the 20,000-step gate to every printed digit). A graphics card would allow running the series on the engine itself.
- A walled-channel blockage series to compare with Sahin and Owens 2004: the engine has no wall edges; walls can only be added as mask rows, as in the Schaefer and Turek case. It would not bear on the page, whose edges are open.
- Tritton 1959 is an experiment with its own wall and end effects; it is listed among the references, and the pass/fail uses the centre of the cited range.
- The 32 D by 32 D domain at Re 200 (budget); the Re 200 domain-free value uses the upstream series at H 16 D, whose height effect at Re 200 is under the spread shown in section 3.1.
- The Schaefer and Turek inlet (a parabola imposed 2 D ahead of the cylinder) on the engine itself: its inflow edge is uniform. The comparison with the benchmark inlet uses a test-only build of the transcription (l2lbm3p.c, option parab=1, every other path identical to l2lbm3.c).
