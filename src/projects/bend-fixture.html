Case study / SunThru Custom three-point bend fixture

Commercial bend fixtures will damage a fragile specimen before the test begins. I designed a fully printable 12 part one for SunThru's samples.

All work
IP protected

Renders are blurred because the fixture is SunThru hardware.

12Interlocking parts
100%FDM printable, no machining
M3One hardware size throughout
VBAAutomated rebuilds

All 12 parts sit on one shared coordinate system. I automated the rebuilds with SolidWorks VBA, so a dimension change regenerates the affected parts.

Horizontal M3 cross-holes are drawn as printable channels, so nothing needs support material or post-drilling. One hardware size across the whole fixture.
