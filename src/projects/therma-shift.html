A thermoelectric module heats or cools depending on current direction. The TEC1-12703 sits between an aluminum top plate and a finned heatsink with a blower.

An NTC thermistor at the plate closes the loop, targeting about 2 degrees F around setpoint.

Separate firmware cutoffs watch for thermal runaway independently of the control loop and shut the device down on a fault.

Before adding any control loop, I bench tested the bare thermal stack on a lab supply with K-type thermocouples on both faces.
