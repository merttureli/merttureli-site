Business cards

Five templates, same tokens as the site: #0A0A0A on #FFFFFF, Space Grotesk over IBM Plex Mono, 0.18em caps tracking. Shown at true size. 3.5 × 2 in trim inside a 3.75 × 2.25 in document. Pink is the trim line, cut happens there. Blue is the safe area, nothing important crosses it. Every back carries a QR pointing at a tracked URL, so a scan lands in your own dashboard.
