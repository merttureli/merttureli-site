Business cards, second pass

Four concepts, not four arrangements. The first set broke the one rule that actually matters in print: 8pt is the floor for anything a person reads, and it had metadata at 6.5pt and labels at 4.6pt. Nothing here is under 8pt. Names 13pt except where scale is the whole idea. Two typefaces, no more. Pink is trim, blue is safe.

A  Specimen

The name is the design. 31pt, filling the width, everything else one quiet line at the foot. This is what confidence looks like on a card: the nerve to leave two thirds of it empty.
