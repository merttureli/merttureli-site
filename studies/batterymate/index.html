How it works

Three steps. Scroll to run them.

  1. 01

    Catch.

    The feeder is a rotating dual chamber that catches the ball.

  2. 02

    Turn.

    The chamber rotates 180 degrees.

  3. 03

    Return.

    It hands the ball to the return flywheel, driven by a 120 W DC motor.
