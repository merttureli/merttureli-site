A size is the sheet. The photograph keeps its own proportions inside a white border, which is where it is numbered and signed.

Two papers, three frames

Cotton rag
Matte, 310 gsm, soft to the touch. Deep blacks and no glare. Every print starts here.
Baryta
Semi-gloss, 315 gsm, close to a darkroom print. Adds $15 at any size.
Frames
Solid wood, 0.75 in wide, in black, oak or white, glazed with clear acrylic. From $90 on the smallest sheet to $240 on the largest.

Packed and sent

  1. Sheets up to 18 × 24 in travel flat between two boards.
  2. The 24 × 36 in sheet is rolled in tissue and sent in a tube.
  3. Framed prints go in a fitted box with corner guards.
  4. Every order is printed within ten days and sent tracked.

About

The shop

Halfstop Editions publishes photographs as limited prints. Twelve are in print at a time. When one sells out it leaves the index and another takes its place.

A half stop is a small step of exposure: the difference between a sky that holds and a sky that burns out. The name is a reminder to make one small correction and then leave the picture alone.

Every print is made to order, checked against a proof under daylight lamps, then numbered and signed in pencil in the border.

Edition rules

  1. Each photograph is printed 50 times across all four sizes, then never again.
  2. Every print is numbered and signed in pencil.
  3. Numbers are given in the order the prints are sold.
  4. A print that arrives damaged is replaced under the same number.
  5. No open editions, no reprints, no posters.
