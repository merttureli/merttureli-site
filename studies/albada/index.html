The morning, hour by hour

06:40The field

It starts
in the dark.

You meet us in a field outside the city while the sky is still blue-black. The burner lights the stubble, and the envelope lies on its side and fills until it is a room you could walk into. There is coffee, and nothing to do yet but watch.

07:15Lift-off

Then the ground
lets go.

There is no runway and no rush. The basket gets light, the field drops away without a sound, and you are looking down at the place you were standing. Other balloons rise around you, dark against the first light.

07:40Over the city

The city,
before it wakes.

The wind decides the route, so no two mornings cross Segovia the same way. On this one the Alcázar passes below on its rock, then the cathedral and the roofs around it. The sun clears the horizon and the whole plain turns gold.

08:10Above the cloud

Above the cloud,
a halo.

Some mornings there is cloud, and the balloon climbs through it into clear sun. Look down and you find your own shadow on the white, with a ring of color around it. It is called a glory: sunlight thrown back by the droplets of the cloud, and it follows you for as long as you stay above it.

Choose your morning

08:40Down in the fields

Down in the wheat,
breakfast.

The pilot picks a field and brings the basket down as gently as it left. The cathedral is still in view, small now, above the roofs. We fold the envelope together and then there is breakfast, on a table in the field: bread, coffee, and a glass of cava if you like.

Six answersQuestions

Before
you fly.

What happens if the weather is bad?

Balloons fly only in calm air. The pilot decides the evening before, and if the flight is off you choose another morning or a full refund.

What should I wear?

Dress as you would for a walk at that hour: layers and flat, closed shoes. The burner is warm overhead, so a hat helps if you are tall.

Can children fly?

Yes, from seven years old, as long as they can see over the edge of the basket. Anyone under sixteen flies with an adult.

How long does it take?

About three and a half hours from the field to the ride back, with about an hour and a half of it in the air. Plan nothing before eleven.

Where do we meet?

In a field outside the city. The wind chooses which one, so you get the place and the exact hour the evening before. Ask and we pick you up in town.

Can I get a refund?

In full up to seven days before your morning, and always if the weather cancels the flight. Inside seven days you can move to another morning once, at no cost.
