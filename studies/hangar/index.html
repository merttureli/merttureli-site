01CHARGERUnion College Rocket Team / 2026

Designed, built and flown to first place.

There was no rocket team at Union College when I started. I founded it, ran it as captain and chief engineer, and led the design of the airframe and the deployable payload.

The event asked for a custom sensor payload dropped at apogee, a nose cone released at 75 percent of peak altitude on the way down, and five channels of telemetry streamed live to a handheld ground station. CHARGER won it at Battle of the Rockets 2026.

  • 1st Deployable Sensor Payload event, Battle of the Rockets 2026.
    1. Union College Rocket Club
    2. Chantilly Aerospace Club Team 3
    3. Thomas Jefferson High School
  • 1.80cal Stability margin on a 37 inch airframe, centre of gravity at 22 inches.
  • 13.8ft/s Descent under a 48 inch canopy, against a 15 ft/s limit.
    0Limit 15
  • $716 Total hardware: rocket $465.42, payload $250.53, ground station from existing kit.

02Selected builds2024 to 2026

Machines I designed, wired and programmed.

Most of my work has the same shape: something needs to exist, nobody has built it yet, and the fastest way to find out if it works is to design it, wire it, write the firmware, and then build the rig that measures it.

  • 02.1Mechanism / Electronics / Firmware

    Robo-Catcher

    2025

    An automatic softball catching and return machine, built by a twelve person capstone team. I co-led the feeding subsystem, then owned all of the electronics and firmware. It hit one hundred percent feed indexing reliability on competition day.

    Case study ↗
  • 02.2Mechanism / Firmware

    Flight sim rudder pedals

    2026

    A three axis rudder and toe brake set, 3D printed, with contactless magnetic sensing. I wrote the USB HID firmware, so it works in any simulator with no drivers.

    Case study ↗
  • 02.3Thermal / Instrumentation IP protected

    HOTBOX thermal bench

    2026

    For SunThru I designed and built the whole bench: chamber, sensor stack, electronics, firmware and a layered safety system for unattended overnight runs. It reads heat flow directly, for roughly a quarter of a commercial quote.

  • 02.4Design for manufacture IP protected

    Three-point bend fixture

    2026

    A fully 3D printable 12 part fixture for aerogel specimens too fragile for commercial fixtures. It is in use for flexural characterization.

    Case study ↗
  • 02.5Thermal / Control electronics

    Therma-Shift coaster

    2026

    A solid state coaster that heats or cools any mug, with closed loop temperature control and thermal runaway cutoffs. I bench validated the thermal core before adding the electronics.

    Case study ↗
