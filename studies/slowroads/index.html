Stop 1 of 6

The road
north

The first pictures are all from the car. Two lanes, a double yellow line, and trees close on both sides with power lines running along. On a bridge the railing went by in a blur, with a few boats sitting on gray water behind it. One sign and the paint on the road agreed with each other: stop ahead.

all through the windshield

Stop 2 of 6

The
lighthouse

A white tower with a black lantern room, and a house with a red roof built right up against it. From the shrubs on the far side, the sea sat flat and pale behind it all the way to the horizon. Up close the house was all gables and arched windows, dark under a heavy sky, and people were walking the path around it.

the only lighthouse in this issue

Stop 3 of 6

The coast

Up close the granite was pink and orange, with grass growing out of every crack. Across the water, spruce came down a low shore and stopped at a pale ledge of rock. One cliff stood dark and stacked in heavy layers, leaning out over the swell.

the water really was this blue

Stop 4 of 6

The pond

Jordan Pond, with the two round hills called the Bubbles sitting at the far end. Wild roses were blooming at the edge, bright pink against the gray stones. The wind had put a chop on the water, and somebody still climbed out on the boulders to get a hand in it.

the stamps on these pages got their pink from the roses

Stop 5 of 6

The
overlook

The trail ran over bare granite between spruce trunks and around boulders, with a blue blaze painted on a tree to follow. Off to one side stood a dead trunk full of holes, on a floor of moss and fallen branches. Then the trees stopped at a ledge, with forest below and the ocean laid flat along the horizon under a lot of cloud.

a good place to sit

Stop 6 of 6

The summit
at sunset

From the top, bare granite ran down toward a long lake, and the low sun threw a shadow behind everything standing. The lakes went silver while the hills around them went dark. Then the sun slid behind a bank of cloud and lit its edges orange over the water.

end of the line
